Connect Gmail, Outlook or IMAP

Connect directly on macOS, Windows or Linux. JobTracker requests read-only mail access. Apple Mail remains an option on macOS.

First-time provider registration

Gmail: enable Gmail API in Google Cloud, configure the consent screen and add your address as a test user, then create an OAuth client of type Desktop app. Enter its client ID and optional client secret. Testing-mode Google authorizations may expire after seven days.

Outlook: register an application in Microsoft Entra for your account type. Under Authentication, add a Mobile and desktop platform with redirect URI http://localhost/oauth/callback and delegated Microsoft Graph permissions User.Read and Mail.Read. Enter the Application (client) ID. No Microsoft client secret is used.

Sign-in opens in your browser. Return here after granting read access. Work or school administrators may need to approve access.

Use your provider’s app password. Servers requiring OAuth should use the Gmail or Outlook connection above. TLS is required; JobTracker never marks messages read or changes mailbox contents.

Choose one account before syncing. Other accounts are excluded.

Apple Mail scans wait while Mail is in the foreground. History is collected gradually in small, resumable batches across folders in your selected account, including archived mail, Sent, Spam and Junk (excluding folders named Trash, Deleted Messages and Drafts). Every company match is saved and viewable before AI classifies its type. Failed analyses can be retried without repeating collection. Desktop notifications alert you to new emails and potential status changes; system notification settings control delivery. Email contents and AI analysis stay on this computer. Direct connections contact only your selected provider. Apple Mail access may require macOS automation permission.

Quiet hours & presentation

Muted alerts are not replayed when rules change. Quiet-hour alerts are held until quiet hours end. Muted items remain in notification history, marked muted; muting never approves a status change. Ordinary alerts wait for classification so job ads cannot notify early. System notification and Focus settings still control delivery.

A home for your data

SQLite commits every successful save to disk. Automatic snapshots keep the latest 50 versions, including before imports and deletions.

Keep a copy on another drive or your backup service to protect against disk loss.

Import backup merges saved records and settings into this app; it does not replace the whole database. Duplicate applications are skipped. Original spreadsheet files are never changed.
