# Job ads and notification preferences — 1.8.0

Pure job ads, job alert digests and generic recruiter pitches now have a separate `job_ad` type. They remain saved but are excluded from the default email query before pagination; Show job ads and the Job ads & recruiter pitches filter reveal them. Dedicated job-alert senders and known advertising language are detected before AI, avoiding premature ordinary notifications and unnecessary classification. The type-only model contract also recognizes ads. Application receipts, actual hiring outcomes and personal recruiter replies are protected from conflicting ad labels. An idempotent migration recategorizes clear existing ads without changing their links, human decisions or application statuses.

Profile & data contains notification preferences, also linked from notification history. Options include a master switch, status-review-only mode, category toggles, never/all/keyword job ad alerts, OR phrase matching (including the Boston Dynamics/humanoid preset), inclusion/exclusion phrases, sender/domain and company mutes, opt-in filtering of status reviews, local quiet hours with optional urgent review bypass, sound, previews, attention and dock/taskbar badge controls. Ordinary alerts wait for classification. Muted alerts retain history and read state, are excluded from unread counts, and are never replayed when rules change. Quiet-hour alerts remain pending for later delivery. Preferences and suppression history survive export/restore. System notification permissions and Focus still govern display; the app must be running to deliver alerts.

The app version is visible in the sidebar and Profile & data and comes from the actual packaged Electron version.

Validation: 222 Python and 74 JavaScript tests, including HTTP save/export/restore/restart; synthetic UI exploration of the default ad filter, explicit ad viewing, keyword preset and persistent review-only mode; packaged macOS startup/mail bridge smoke and code-signature verification. Actual local Qwen 3.5 9B passed seven synthetic examples (job alert, recruiter pitch, personal recruiter reply, conditional receipt, interview, technical assessment and rejection). A migration rehearsal preserved all 35 application rows, 178 raw email bodies, 200 links and 75 human review states in a consistent copy of the live database, identifying 42 saved ads. No actual application status was approved as a test and no real email was sent to a model or external service.

Representative tests do not guarantee perfect classification of arbitrary language. Ambiguous correspondence remains subject to review; muting never approves or changes an application status.
